# Contributing

RegelRecht is open source so that anyone can read how Dutch law is translated
into executable rules and check that translation against the law itself. The
project is not organised around outside contributions.

## Issues

An issue is the channel we can act on. Corpus issues are worth the most: a law
modelled incorrectly, a condition that is missing, an interpretation that does
not match the legal text. Name the law, the article, and what the text
requires. You do not have to phrase it as a change to the code.

Questions about the schema, the engine or an RFC also belong in an issue, or as
a comment on the RFC.

## Pull requests

We generally do not merge them. Reviewing an outside change costs us more time
than making the change ourselves, and much of what you would touch is still
being redesigned.

Open one anyway and we will read it. The likely outcome is an issue that
records your point, with the change made separately.

## Legal expertise

If you work on legislation or public administration and you see something that
is legally wrong, an issue helps us more than a patch. Describe what the law
requires and where our model departs from it.

## Reuse
